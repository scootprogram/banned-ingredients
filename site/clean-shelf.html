      How this list is made

      Every beauty and household product in Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts was checked against the list above. Only products sold in the US whose ingredients (or, for household items, materials) pass every check appear here.

      Packaging verified plastic-free means every packaging part recorded for the product is a known plastic-free material. Anything unclear keeps a product out of this group, for example:

      • a jar or bottle listed without its lid or cap (lids are usually plastic-lined)
      • tubes, pumps, sprays, droppers and applicators
      • metal tins and cans, which are lined or lacquered with plastic
      • liquids and creams listed only as "paper" or "cardboard", since the bottle or jar inside isn't listed
      • anything named as sachets, capsules or pouches

      Packaging not verified yet means the product itself is clean, but its packaging hasn't been recorded fully enough to say. If you know a product's packaging, use its Help button. For each question, the most common answer from visitors is shown and the rest are set aside.

      The list depends on what volunteers recorded, so check the label before you buy.
