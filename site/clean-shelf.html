      How this list is made

      Every product in Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts and Open Pet Food Facts was checked against the ban list. A product appears here only if its ingredients were checked and every packaging part listed for it is a known plastic-free material. Anything unclear, such as a bottle listed without its cap, a bare "metal" container or a "pouch", is left out.

      Many glass jars and bottles are recorded as just "glass", with no lid or cap. Those lids are usually plastic-lined, so these items are hidden unless you switch them on, and they are marked when shown.

      The list depends on what volunteers recorded. If a record leaves out a part of the packaging (for example the bag inside a box), the product can still appear here, so check the label before you buy.
