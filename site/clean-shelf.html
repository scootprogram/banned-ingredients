      How this list is made

      Every beauty and household product in Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts was checked against the ban list. A product appears here only if its ingredients or materials were checked and every packaging part listed for it is a known plastic-free material. Anything unclear is left out, for example:

      • a jar or bottle listed without its lid or cap (lids are usually plastic-lined)
      • tubes, pumps, sprays, droppers and applicators
      • metal tins and cans, which are lined or lacquered with plastic
      • liquids and creams listed only as "paper" or "cardboard", since the bottle or jar inside isn't listed
      • anything named as sachets, capsules or pouches

      The list depends on what volunteers recorded. If a record leaves out a part of the packaging (for example the wrapper inside a box), the product can still appear here, so check the label before you buy.
